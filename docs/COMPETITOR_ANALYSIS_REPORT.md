## 1. Executive summary

smtools sits in the **AI social content + publishing** market: generate caption and image, queue/schedule posts, publish to Facebook/LinkedIn pages, plus video rewrite and (planned) audio.

**Closest competitors** are tools that combine **AI creative generation** with **scheduling/auto-post** (Predis.ai, Ocoya, Publer, FeedHive). Broader rivals are classic schedulers with lighter AI (Buffer, Hootsuite, Flick).

**smtools opportunity**
- Cheaper, focused packaging for **page-based posting** (Facebook Page + LinkedIn company)
- Strong fit for **Sinhala / local language** and SME “set and forget” auto posting
- Clear **credit / quota** model (posts + videos + audios) that matches real AI costs

**Main risk**
- Global AI creative tools already offer captions, images, carousels, and video at scale
- smtools must win on **price clarity, language fit, page auto-post reliability, and simplicity** — not on being “another Buffer”

## 3. Market context

### 3.1 What buyers want
1. **Content without a designer** — captions + visuals  
2. **Consistency** — schedule / auto-post so the page stays active  
3. **Multi-platform** — IG, TikTok, FB, LinkedIn (global expectation)  
4. **Affordable AI** — transparent credits or flat plans  
5. **Local language & payments** — especially outside US/EU  

### 3.2 Market segments

| Segment | Need | Who wins today |
|---|---|---|
| Solo creator / SME | Cheap, simple, auto-post | Buffer free, Predis Core, local tools |
| Growing brand | AI creative + schedule | Predis Rise, Ocoya, Publer Business |
| Agency | Multi-account, seats, workflows | Hootsuite, Ocoya Agency, FeedHive |
| Local SMEs (e.g. Sri Lanka) | LKR, Sinhala, FB-first | PostKesho-type locals + DIY |

smtools is strongest in **SME / page operators** who care about **Facebook + LinkedIn page auto posting with AI visuals**, not full social inbox suites.

## 7. SWOT — smtools

### Strengths
- End-to-end **write → image → queue → post to Page**
- Transparent **post / video / audio quotas** tied to real AI COGS
- Language/tone controls (incl. Sinhala) and page voice
- Lower proposed ARPU than Predis/Ocoya while bundling video

### Weaknesses
- Fewer networks than Buffer/Predis (IG/TikTok gap)
- Smaller brand trust vs Hootsuite/Buffer
- Video is **rewrite/duplicate**, not full Predis-style video studio
- Team/agency features thinner than enterprise tools

### Opportunities
- Dominate **Facebook/LinkedIn page autopilot** for SMEs
- Local markets (LKR/Sinhala/payment methods) if expanded
- Credit packs for overage (high margin)
- TTS bundle as differentiator vs Publer/Buffer

### Threats
- Predis/Ocoya price cuts or free tiers
- Meta API policy / Page token friction
- Google AI price increases (esp. video)
- Local schedulers undercutting on price alone

## 8. Competitive strategy recommendations

### 8.1 Positioning
**Primary:** “AI posts for your Facebook & LinkedIn **pages** — caption, image, schedule, done.”  
**Secondary:** Affordable video rewrite + voiceover credits.

Avoid competing with Hootsuite on inbox/analytics depth.

### 8.2 Packaging
1. Keep **Free** as trial funnel (limited posts).  
2. Keep **Basic ~$9** as impulse SME plan.  
3. Sell **Pro ~$19** as default (best value vs Predis Core).  
4. Use **Business ~$39** for power users; upsell **credit packs** for extra video.  
5. Prefer **credits under the hood** even if UI shows posts/videos/audios.

### 8.3 Must-win features (next 2–3 quarters)
| Priority | Feature | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Rock-solid FB/LinkedIn publish + clear failures | Trust |
| P0 | Usage meter (posts/videos/audios left) | Matches competitors’ credit clarity |
| P1 | TTS in packages | Differentiator |
| P1 | Instagram (or IG via Meta) | Table stakes vs Predis/Buffer |
| P2 | Brand kit (logo/colors) | Parity with Predis/Ocoya |
| P2 | Simple analytics | Parity with mid-market |

### 8.4 Pricing defense
- If Predis Core undercuts: emphasize **auto-post included** + video/audio quotas.  
- If Buffer undercuts: emphasize **true image generation**, not rewrite-only AI.  
- Never race Hootsuite on features; race on **time-to-first-scheduled AI post**.

## 9. Financial takeaway for leadership

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can smtools undercut Predis/Ocoya? | **Yes**, at $9–$39 with focused scope |
| Is video the margin risk? | **Yes** — each 10s video ≈ 16× one post in AI cost |
| Should packages stay video-light? | **Yes** relative to posts; sell extra video via credits |
| Is text (title/description/caption) a pricing problem? | **No** — use flat include / small credit; meter **images & videos** |
| Biggest competitive gap | **Instagram/TikTok + brand creative formats (carousels)** |

## 11. One-page conclusion

smtools competes in a crowded field but can own a clear niche: **low-cost AI autopilot for business Pages**, with bundled **posts + limited videos + audios**, priced under Predis/Ocoya and far under Hootsuite.

**Do not win by:** matching every network and every enterprise feature.
